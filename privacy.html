Last updated: June 1, 2025  ·  Applies to: BoxofBox (com.D3IN.BoxofBox)

1. Introduction

AzogCorp ("we", "our", "us") built BoxofBox as a free-to-play mobile game. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it. By playing BoxofBox, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information. However, third-party SDKs integrated in the game may collect the following automatically:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device type, OS version, language
  • Gameplay events and session duration
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Approximate location (country/region level)

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — in-game advertising. Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — leaderboards, achievements. Privacy Policy
  • Unity Analytics / Ads — analytics and advertising. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) — crash reporting, analytics. Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Data

  • Improve game performance and fix crashes
  • Understand how players interact with the game
  • Show relevant advertisements

5. Children's Privacy

BoxofBox is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Retention

We retain analytics and crash data for up to 24 months. Ad-related data is controlled by the respective ad network and subject to their retention policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. You can also opt out of personalized ads via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the game after changes means you accept the updated policy. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
